AUTHORIZED SECURITY TESTING ONLY

This kit launches a self-extracting installer under test and may change system
state (environment variables, temporary files, registry values under the Adobe
ARM key, and a benign proof-of-concept MSI). Run it only on systems you own or
are explicitly authorized to test, and preferably after a snapshot/backup.

The included proof-of-concept MSI is benign: it installs a small executable
(payload/poc.exe) whose only action is to write C:\poc-marker.txt as a deferred,
no-impersonate custom action, demonstrating elevated code execution.

Do not redistribute the bundled Adobe binaries (payload/setup.exe) or the
installer. The installer is supplied by the tester and is not committed.
